# Earth texture sources

Downloaded and verified 2026-10-09. Both JPEGs are 4096 x 2048 equirectangular maps. Neither is AI generated.

- `earth_day_4096.jpg` (473093 bytes): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/planets/earth_day_4096.jpg
- `earth_night_4096.jpg` (267061 bytes): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/dev/examples/textures/planets/earth_night_4096.jpg

Provenance: the official three.js Earth example credits Solar System Scope for these Earth textures (resized and merged): https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/webgpu_tsl_earth.html

Original author/source: Solar System Scope / INOVE, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

Recommended site credit: Earth textures by Solar System Scope / INOVE, CC BY 4.0; adapted by three.js (resized and merged).

The source describes the Earth maps as assembled from geographic data, space photos and NASA Blue Marble imagery, with tuned colors and seasonal vegetation/snow coverage. They are suitable for illustrative globes; they are not live satellite observations.
